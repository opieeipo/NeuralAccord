Neural Accord · Reading the constraints

What a model is allowed to conclude

Neural Accord's central discipline is not how good its numbers are. It is keeping straight what was measured, what was modelled, what was abstracted, and what is merely hoped. The fidelity ladder is how that discipline is enforced: each level fixes a ceiling on the claims a run at that level may support, no matter how the results look.

The fidelity ladder

Select a level to see what it permits, which constraints are in play, and which hypotheses it can bear on. A report that draws a Level 2 conclusion from a Level 0 run is a defect, not a stretch.

What is implemented

    Bears on

    Four labels, in descending confidence

    Every parameter and every mechanism in the project carries one of these. The labels are ordinal — they rank how much weight a number can bear — which is why they are drawn as a progression rather than as four colours of the same standing.

    Measured

    Direct observation in a named species, circuit, connection class, and condition. The only label that describes something someone actually recorded.

    Modelled

    A published model fitted to or constrained by empirical data. Inherits the fit's assumptions along with its numbers.

    Abstracted

    A deliberate simplification, with its retained and omitted properties written down. Most of Level 0 lives here.

    Hypothesis

    An untested prediction of the project's own. Never represented as settled fact, and never quietly promoted by being repeated.

    No configuration may use an undocumented “mammalian default.” A missing human parameter is recorded as unknown, inferred, or mouse_prior_for_sensitivity_only — never silently copied from the mouse profile and presented as a human measurement.

    Where each mechanism stops being a biological claim

    The limitation column is the load-bearing one. It is what keeps a convenient abstraction from being read as a finding about brains.

    Six hypotheses, each with a way to lose

    A hypothesis with no declared falsifying outcome is not ready to be listed. Each card states the claim and the result that would kill it.

    Two species, kept apart on purpose

    Mouse and human evidence live in separate profiles and are never averaged into an undocumented mammalian default. Comparative work holds the task and the analysis constant and changes only the documented species constraints.

    Baseline

    Mouse cortex

    The strongest experimentally tractable mammalian foundation for linking cell type, connectivity, synaptic physiology, plasticity, behaviour, and perturbation.

    Constraint

    Human cortex

    Tests transfer rather than assuming mouse is an interchangeable human proxy. Viable human tissue is more limited, so more entries here legitimately stay unknown.

    Transfer

    Comparative

    Pairs the two so one experiment runs twice under documented, differing constraints, and reports which findings survive.

    What Neural Accord does not claim

    • That a symbolic channel is a literal synapse.
    • That it simulates an entire brain.
    • That it establishes a theory of consciousness.
    • That a Level 3 model of a named circuit is a model of cognition.
    • That an abstraction becomes a measurement by being useful.

    Collapse regimes are reported with the same care as successes. A sweep that finds only failure is a result; a sweep whose failing arm goes unreported is a defect.
