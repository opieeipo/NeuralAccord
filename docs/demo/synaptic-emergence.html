Convention formation under a bounded, unreliable channel

Two systems have to agree on what a symbol means, using a channel that drops some transmissions outright and corrupts others. Nobody hands them a protocol. This page runs that experiment live — including the regimes where it fails.

This is an abstraction, not a measurement. A token here is not a vesicle and this channel is not a synapse. Level 0 licenses exactly one kind of claim: the computational effects of constrained stochastic communication. Nothing on this page is a reportable result — it has no seed list, no variance across seeds, and no resolved configuration. It is an illustration of the mechanism the Python implementation will own.

What the model actually does

Each episode, the sender observes one of K meanings drawn uniformly and emits a symbol from a vocabulary of V. The channel then does one of three things to that symbol, and the three are kept deliberately distinct:

  • Delivered — the receiver sees what was sent.
  • Silence — the transmission is erased. The receiver observes a labelled absence. That label is a diagnostic control, not a biological claim: real postsynaptic systems get no notice of a failed release. Level 2 removes the label.
  • Substituted — a different symbol arrives, standing in for competing activity, timing jitter, and background noise.

The receiver picks one of K actions. Both sides are tabular softmax policies updated by a reward-modulated rule with a running baseline. Neither is told what any symbol means.

Why private observation excludes the update

When the receiver independently observes the state, it answers correctly no matter what was transmitted. Rewarding the sender on those episodes would credit it for a signal that did no work — textbook reward leakage. So private episodes count toward reported accuracy but are withheld from both policy updates. The gap between Accuracy and Comms only is exactly the share of success the channel didn't earn.

Things worth trying

  • Push erasure past 0.6 at budget 1 and watch coordination sit near chance — the collapse regime is a result, not a failure to report.
  • Then set budget to 2. Under heavy erasure the sender often learns to spend both slots on the same symbol. Redundancy emerging from unreliable transmission is what H1 predicts, and you can watch it happen.
  • Set vocabulary below meanings. Some meanings must share a symbol; the lexicon shows which ones the pair decided to confuse.
  • Raise private observation to 0.5. Overall accuracy climbs while comms-only accuracy sags — the pair stops needing the channel. That intermediate band is where H2 says boundary behaviour should appear.
  • Change only the seed. The lexicon that forms is different every time; the fact that one forms is the robust finding, which is why a single run is never reportable.
